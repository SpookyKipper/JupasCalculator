Short Description

This programme aims to equip students with the theoretical, technical and professional skills and knowledge in horticulture and arboriculture management, landscape construction and maintenance, related sciences of plants, arboriculture and the environment, and sustainable landscape management.Â  It will prepare students to perform the roles and tasks related to horticultural and arboricultural supervision, tree assessment, landscape management, and development of landscapes and conservation of the environment.

First Year Tuition FeeUpdated

HK$ 115,590

Tuition Fees for the 2027/28 Cohort are as follows for reference:

Before SSSDP Subsidy

  • First-Year Tuition Fee: HK$115,590
  • Estimated Total Programme Fee (4-year normal duration): HK$462,360


After Deducting SSSDP Subsidy

  • First-Year Tuition Fee: HK$63,710
  • Estimated Total Programme Fee (4-year normal duration): HK$254,840


Remarks:

  1. Tuition fees for subsequent years are subject to annual adjustment, capped at the movement of the Composite Consumer Price Index (CCPI) announced by the Government. The estimated total programme fee is calculated based on the tuition fee for the first year of study and has not yet reflected any CCPI-based fee adjustments for subsequent academic years (i.e. AY2028/29 onwards).
  2. The annual subsidy for this programme is HK$51,880 for the 2027/28 cohort. The subsidy is tenable for the normal duration of the programme, i.e. 4 years.
  3. Only applicants who satisfy the Category I (CAT I) eligibility criteria stipulated by the Education Bureau (EDB) are eligible for the Study Subsidy Scheme for Designated Professions/Sectors (SSSDP). For details of the Scheme and the eligibility criteria, please visit: https://www.cspe.edu.hk/sssdp.
