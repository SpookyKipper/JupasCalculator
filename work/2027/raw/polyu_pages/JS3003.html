Aims & Characteristics

At PolyU, innovation and technology drive an academic environment that fosters national pride, social responsibility, and a global mindset. Our Bachelor's Degree Scheme gives you the time and flexibility to explore various fields, helping you make informed decisions about your preferred programme.

 

This Bachelor's Degree Scheme is an admission pathway offered and operated at the Faculty level alongside our existing departmental level BBA Schemes offered across our three departments. It provides students with foundational knowledge through a broad one-year curriculum that covers core business functions.

 

The Faculty-level Bachelor's Degree Scheme offers students maximum flexibility to develop individual study paths that match their interests, aspirations, and learning needs. Students will take a range of subjects in their first year of study before making a decision on the appropriate BBA Major across our three departments.

Curriculum

All admitted students embark on a Common Year One curriculum. Towards the end of year one, students can choose their preferred programme through a ranking assessment, which considers a combination of entrance qualification score, Year One GPA, and interview performance.

 

All programmes under the departmental BBA (Hons) Schemes have the following structure:

  • General University Requirements (GUR)

  • Compulsory Core subjects for all programmes under the BBA (Hons) Scheme

  • Major subjects

  • Free Electives

 

For details on the specific curriculum of the programmes, please refer to the respective entries of the three departmental BBA (Hons) Schemes.

Secondary Major Details

Some of our programmes offer the opportunity to pursue a Secondary Major (AI and Data Analytics (AIDA)), allowing students to further broaden their academic and professional horizons. 

For details, please refer to the respective entries of the three departmental BBA (Hons) Schemes (see also the Curriculum section above).
